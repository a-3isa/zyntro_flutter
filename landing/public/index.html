The idea

Workouts don't die in the gym.

6:47pm

The moment most workouts actually end — on the couch, before they start. Zyntro exists for that moment.

Skipping alone is easy. Nobody sees it. Nobody asks. Tomorrow becomes next week and next week becomes January.

But when your friend is already warming up — when their timer is running on your screen and their voice note says "you coming or what?" — skipping stops being an option.

Zyntro puts you in the same room even when you're miles apart. Not a feed you scroll later. Not a chart you check once. A live session, happening right now, with someone who's counting on you.

That's the whole idea. Accountability that feels like hanging out.

How it works

Three taps to the same room.

No group chats. No "you still coming?". Just invite, go live, and lift.

Invite your buddy

Build your plan from a full exercise library and send the invite. They get a push, tap, and pick their own workout.

Go live together

Once everyone's ready, the room goes live. A shared clock, a live activity strip, every set landing the moment it's logged.

Keep showing up

Finish with a recap you both earned. Streaks grow, your circle climbs the board, and next session is already on the calendar.
