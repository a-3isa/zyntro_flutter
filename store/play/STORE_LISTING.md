## Full description  (≤4000)

```
Working out alone is easy to skip. Zyntro fixes that.

Zyntro turns your solo workout into a LIVE session with your friends. Invite a
buddy, start the room, and train together in real time — every set, rep and
second synced. When someone's counting on you, you show up.

WHY ZYNTRO

• Live sessions, in real time
  Start a room and your friend drops in instantly. A shared clock, a live
  activity strip, and every set the moment it lands — you always know exactly
  where your partner is.

• See every rep
  Live weight, reps and set time for every athlete in the room. Friendly
  pressure that keeps you both honest.

• Invite in one tap
  Send a session invite, your buddy accepts, picks their plan, and you go live
  together. No group chats, no "you still coming?".

• Voice notes mid-set
  Hold to talk and drop a "one more rep" straight into your partner's session
  while they lift.

• Build your workout in seconds
  Search a full exercise database, pick your sets, and jump straight into the
  room. Gym runs turn-based sets; cardio and timed workouts share a live clock.

• No friend free? Train with a bot
  Nobody around? Pair with a pace-setting bot partner, or let matchmaking find
  someone training right now — a session never has to wait.

• Streaks & leaderboards
  Keep your run alive and climb with your circle. Progress your friends can
  actually see.

BUILT FOR EVERYONE
Lifters, runners, and everyone in between. Gym, cardio, and time-based
workouts all work out of the box.

Grab your buddy. Start the session. Make training the thing you both show up
for.

Sign in with email, Google, or Facebook and you're in the room in seconds.
```
