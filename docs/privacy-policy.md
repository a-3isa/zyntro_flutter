# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 1, 2026**

## Introduction

Zyntro ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and share information about you when you use our mobile application.

Engineering inventory for store declarations: [`DATA_INVENTORY.md`](./DATA_INVENTORY.md).

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Account Information**: Email address, display name, profile photo
- **Workout Data**: Exercise logs, session data, workout statistics
- **Content**: Voice notes, photos, posts, and comments you create
- **Social Connections**: Friends list, follow relationships

### Information Collected Automatically
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, unique identifiers
- **Usage Data**: Features used, session duration, app interactions
- **Push Notification Data**: FCM tokens for delivering notifications

## How We Use Your Information

- Provide and improve our services
- Enable social features (buddies, following, leaderboards)
- Send push notifications for session updates and activities
- Analyze usage patterns to improve the app
- Ensure platform safety and enforce community guidelines

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:
- **Service Providers**: Firebase (Google), Stripe for payment processing
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law or to protect rights

## Data Retention

We retain your data while your account is active. Delete your account in the app (**Settings → Delete account**) or follow [Data Deletion Instructions](./data-deletion.md). Email privacy@zyntro.app for help.

## Meta / Facebook Login

If you use Facebook Login, Meta requires a published data-deletion URL. Use the in-app route `/legal/data-deletion` or host `docs/data-deletion.md` at your public site and paste that URL into the Meta App Dashboard.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your account and data
- Export your workout data

## Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:
- Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
- Secure authentication via Firebase Auth
- Firestore security rules restricting data access

## Children's Privacy

Zyntro is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes through the app.

## Contact Us

For privacy-related inquiries:
- Email: privacy@zyntro.app
