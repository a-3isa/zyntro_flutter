# Data Deletion Instructions

**Effective: September 2026**  
**Public URL for Meta App Dashboard:** use the in-app route `/legal/data-deletion` (deep link) or host this document at `https://zyntro.app/data-deletion`.

## Delete from the Zyntro app

1. Sign in to Zyntro.
2. Open **Settings → Delete account**.
3. Confirm. We permanently remove your profile, workouts, posts, follows, blocks, notification prefs, and profile media.

## Facebook Login users

1. Optionally revoke Zyntro under Facebook **Settings → Apps and Websites**.
2. Still use **Delete account** in Zyntro (or email us) so server-side data is erased — revoking Facebook access alone does not wipe Zyntro storage.

## Email request

Email **privacy@zyntro.app** with subject `Data deletion request` and the email / Facebook user id on the account. Verified requests are completed within **30 days**.

## What we delete

- Account profile and username reservation
- Workout history and session participation records
- Posts, comments, reactions, and uploaded photos
- Follow / block edges and notification preferences
- Profile photos in cloud storage

De-identified aggregates that cannot identify you may be retained for analytics and abuse prevention.
