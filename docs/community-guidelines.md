# Community Guidelines

**Welcome to Zyntro!**

Our community is built on mutual support, motivation, and respect. These guidelines help ensure everyone has a positive experience.

## Core Values

### 🤝 Be Supportive
- Encourage your workout buddies and fellow users
- Celebrate others' achievements and progress
- Offer constructive feedback when asked

### 💪 Be Authentic
- Share genuine workout experiences
- Post accurate workout data
- Be honest in your interactions

### 🙏 Be Respectful
- Treat all users with dignity and respect
- Respect differences in fitness levels and goals
- Accept that everyone's fitness journey is unique

## Content Guidelines

### ✅ Encouraged Content
- Workout updates and progress photos
- Motivational voice notes
- Exercise tips and form advice
- Personal fitness achievements
- Supportive comments and reactions

### ❌ Prohibited Content
- Harassment, bullying, or threats
- Hate speech or discrimination
- Sexually explicit content
- Dangerous or harmful advice
- Spam or promotional content
- Misleading health/fitness claims
- Impersonation of others
- Personal information of others (doxxing)

## Voice Notes

Voice notes are a key feature of Zyntro. When sending voice notes:
- Keep them positive and motivational
- Avoid offensive language
- Respect that others may be in shared spaces

## Profile Guidelines

- Use an appropriate profile photo
- Choose a respectful display name
- Don't impersonate other users or public figures

## Reporting Violations

If you encounter content or behavior that violates these guidelines:
1. Tap the report button on the content or profile
2. Select the type of violation
3. Our moderation team will review within 24 hours

## Enforcement

Violations may result in:
- Content removal
- Warning notification
- Temporary account suspension
- Permanent account ban

The severity of action depends on the nature and frequency of violations.

## Appeals

If you believe an action was taken in error, contact us at: support@zyntro.app

## Questions?

For questions about these guidelines, reach out to our support team.
